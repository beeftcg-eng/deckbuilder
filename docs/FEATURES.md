# Features

Everything Beef's Brewhouse does, in more detail than the README. The patch notes in the app (Patch notes, at the bottom of the sidebar) say what changed in each version.


- **Card database** synced from each game's community API (see [DATA-SOURCES.md](DATA-SOURCES.md)), cached locally so you can browse and build offline after the first sync.
- **Deckbuilding** with per-game zones (e.g. Leader + Main for One Piece; Legend + Main + Runes + Battlefields + Sideboard for Riftbound; a single 60-card deck for Pokémon; Main Deck + Sideboard for Magic's 60-card formats, or Commander + a 99-card Main Deck for Commander), enforced copy limits, and color/domain lock.
- **Legality checking** against each game's format rules (Pokémon Standard/Expanded/Unlimited via the official per-card legality data; Magic (Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Commander) via Scryfall's per-card legality data, including Vintage's restricted list and Commander's singleton rule and color identity; One Piece and Riftbound via an editable local ban list — see below).
- **Collection tracking**: mark how many copies of each card you own (the **Own** stepper on every card, or **✓ I own this deck** / **✓ Got it** on the wishlist). Decks then show what you're still missing (`own 1/4`), the browser can filter to **Owned** cards, and **☆ Wishlist missing** adds only the cards you neither own nor have already wishlisted. Owning any printing of a card counts (by name for Riftbound/Pokémon; by card number for One Piece, matching each game's copy-limit rule).
- **Card wishlist**: star any card (or a whole deck at once, via the "Wishlist deck" button) to track cards you still need, across all three games. Optionally connect a [Pawmodoro](https://github.com/beeftcg-eng/pawmodoro) account to push unpushed wishlist cards straight into its checklist, in their own section separate from your regular tasks. It's pre-connected to the shared Pawmodoro project — just enter an email and password (**Create account** the first time, or the same login you use in Pawmodoro).
- **Deck tools**: import a decklist from pasted text (a line that gives only a card's name gets the printing you own, else one that's legal in the chosen format, else the regular one over alternate art / a promo, else the lowest rarity — never just whichever the card data lists first) (this app's own export, or common community formats such as `4xOP01-006` / `4 Professor's Research SVI 189`), duplicate a deck, sort/filter the deck list, multi-level **undo** (Ctrl+Z, or the button in the sidebar) for card changes, deck deletes and creations, a cost curve / type / color breakdown, and a **Sample hand** simulator (draw an opening hand, mulligan, draw more).
- **Tournament record from Pairings**: link a deck to [Pairings](https://pairings-coral.vercel.app), the TCG tournament tracker, and its deck view shows the record you've logged with it there (wins-losses-draws, win rate, number of events, the opponents it beats and loses to most, and each event with its placement and record), and My Decks shows each deck's W-L. Results refresh when you come back to the window (at most once a minute) or with ↻. **🏆 Connect Pairings** in the sidebar (or **🏆 Tournament record: connect Pairings** in a deck view) walks through it as three steps: log in here with your Pairings account; in Pairings, go to **Stats → Manage your decks → Connect Brewhouse** (log in with this app's Pawmodoro account) and import your decks; then log results with them there (on an event, or **Stats → Log a result** for one that isn't on the calendar or a testing session). It only reads your results. Every save also records a fingerprint of the deck's card list (`summary.listHash`). When a linked deck's list changes, Pairings starts a new version of it, so results can be split by version. Until Pairings has synced the change, the deck view and editor show a reminder with **Open Pairings to sync ↗**. Pairings' **Open in Brewhouse ↗** opens the deck in the phone app (`?deck=<id>`). To make that work, every save also stores a short readable `summary` on the deck (legend/leader, colors, card count, format), since Pairings can't read card ids.
- **Type and Kind filters** in the card browser, as chips: Yu-Gi-Oh! Monster/Spell/Trap and Fusion/Synchro/Xyz/Link/Ritual/Pendulum...; Pokémon Pokémon/Trainer/Energy and Basic/Stage 1/Stage 2/Item/Supporter/Stadium...; Magic Creature/Instant/Sorcery/Enchantment/Artifact... (a card with two types shows under both); Riftbound Unit/Spell/Gear/Battlefield/Legend/Rune. Chips in one row widen the search, the two rows narrow each other. Cards that don't go in the main deck (Riftbound runes, One Piece leaders and DON!! cards, Yu-Gi-Oh! tokens) stay out of the list until you tick their type or name it in the search ("rune", "DON!!"), which is how to find the alt-art runes and DON!! cards.
- **Overnumbered** is a Kind chip for Riftbound, next to Champion and Signature: Overnumbered printings share the Showcase rarity with alternate arts, so the rarity filter can't pick them out.
- **Enlarge a card**: click the picture in a card's details to see it full size, then **Copy image** (paste it anywhere) or **Save image…**.
- **Search** matches a card's name, rules text and types, and also its expansion: the set's name ("Legacy of Destruction", "Paldea Evolved") or code ("LEDE", "OP01"), or the full printed code ("LEDE-EN067", "SVI 189"). Several words match in any order ("don gold", "rune alternate"), in every game.
- **Sort** the card browser by best match, name (A–Z / Z–A), newest or oldest release, expansion (set, then card number), cost or price, either way; the choice is remembered on the device (`src/shared/cardSort.ts`). Release dates come from each game's card data (`Card.released`): Magic per printing, Pokémon and Riftbound per set, Yu-Gi-Oh! the card's first TCG release. One Piece's source has no dates, so it has no date sorts. Cards cached before 0.38.0 need one **Update card data** for the date sorts.
- **Moving cards**: **Move…** on a binder row or a deck-editor row moves copies to another binder or deck of that game, in any direction (they leave where they were; your collection doesn't change).
- **Yu-Gi-Oh! artworks**: a printing shows the artwork it was actually printed with, and offers only that printing's artworks (e.g. Monster Reborn 26LP-EN001 shows its World Championship art; Dark Magician GFTP-EN128 has just the one). YGOPRODeck doesn't record which printing uses which art, so `scripts/build-ygo-art-map.py` works it out from Yugipedia's printing photos and writes `src/shared/games/yugiohArtMap.ts` (re-run it now and then for new sets; ~986 printings mapped). Printings it isn't sure about keep offering every artwork, with a **🎨 N arts** badge to pick the one on your copy.
- **Prices**: every game shows a market price (USD), with deck value and "to buy" totals. One Piece uses optcgapi's `market_price` and Magic Scryfall's USD price. Riftbound, Pokémon and Yu-Gi-Oh! use TCGplayer market prices from tcgcsv.com's daily dump: `scripts/build-prices.ts` writes them to `prices/<game>.json` on the phone app's site whenever it's deployed, and daily (`.github/workflows/deploy-pwa.yml`), since tcgcsv doesn't let a web page read it directly. They're matched by TCGplayer product id (Riftbound; set + printed number for a new set the card data has no ids for yet), set code/id + number (Pokémon, promos by their number) and printed code + rarity (Yu-Gi-Oh!), see `src/shared/priceKeys.ts`. Coverage when added: Riftbound 98% (all but Metal promos TCGplayer has no price for), Pokémon 94% (was 13%), Yu-Gi-Oh! 98% (was 34%). Pokémon's pokemontcg.io prices and YGOPRODeck's set prices remain the fallback. **Riftbound printings riftcodex lacks**: the same build publishes TCGplayer's Riftbound printings (`prices/riftbound-printings.json`), and a sync adds the ones riftcodex doesn't have (Nexus Night, Top 8/Champion and Prize Wall promos, Signature cards, most runes, a set that isn't out yet) as cards, borrowing text and type from the regular printing (`src/shared/games/riftboundExtras.ts`); copies of one move to riftcodex's card once it lists the printing (`cardIdRepair.ts`). TCGplayer has pictures for some of them; the rest show a placeholder. Cards cached before need one **Update card data** to pick prices up. **Daily updates**: prices refresh by themselves: when the app opens (and when you come back to it after 6 hours or more) it applies the day's published prices to every game you've downloaded, without re-downloading the cards (`src/shared/priceRefresh.ts`, `cards.refreshPrices`). The daily build also publishes One Piece and Magic prices, from the app's own download code bundled with rolldown, keyed by card id. **Currency**: pick one under Language in the menu (US dollar, Mexican peso, euro, Canadian dollar, pound, real, yen, Australian dollar; a Mexican-Spanish system starts in pesos). Prices are converted from US dollars at the day's European Central Bank rate (frankfurter.dev), published as `prices/rates.json` by the same daily build; the app keeps the last rates for offline use (`src/shared/currency.ts`).
- **Ban list editor**: for One Piece and Riftbound, *Edit ban list…* under the legality panel edits banned cards, restricted cards, banned pairs and the legal-set rotation in the app, and records when the list was last reviewed (it turns amber after 60 days).
- **Deck view**: selecting a deck opens it as a finished deck filling the window — **Grid** (card images with ×N badges, a card-size slider, grouped by card type), **List** (compact rows with cost, type and price) or **Text** (the plain decklist in columns, with **Copy list**). Click **✎ Edit deck** to get to the editor (and **⛶ View deck** there to come back). Click any card for its details; **⛶ Full screen** hands the window to the OS's real full screen. Your last-used view is remembered. New decks and duplicates open in the editor, since you're about to fill them.
- **Share a deck by link**: **🔗 Share** in the deck view makes a link (`https://beeftcg-eng.github.io/deckbuilder/?share=<token>`) anyone can open on any device, signed in or not. They see the deck read-only ("Shared by" your trade display name, never your email) and can **＋ Copy to my decks**; later edits show up for them, and **Stop sharing** (or deleting the deck) kills the link. Needs the Pawmodoro account (the deck has to be in the cloud) and Pawmodoro's `supabase/schema.sql` from 2026-09-28 or later (`deckbuilder_deck_shares`, `deckbuilder_share_deck` / `deckbuilder_unshare_deck` / `deckbuilder_shared_deck`). Pasting a share link into **Import** opens it too, which is how the desktop app opens one. Logic: `src/shared/deckShare.ts`; screens: `ShareDeckModal.tsx`, `SharedDeckView.tsx`.
- **Collection value over time**: the Collection panel saves what your collection of each game is worth once a day (the last value of the day wins) and draws it as a small graph with the change over 30 days / 90 days / a year / everything. Kept in the app settings on each device (`AppSettings.valueHistory`, about a year of points per game). Logic: `src/shared/valueHistory.ts`; graph: `ValueChart.tsx`.
- **Price over time**: a card's details show its market price over the last 90 days (Riftbound, One Piece and Pokémon). The daily price build adds each day's prices to `prices/history/<game>/<shard>.json` on the phone app's site, starting from the history already there, so the app fetches one small file per card (`src/shared/priceHistory.ts`). Magic and Yu-Gi-Oh! have too many printings to keep 90 days of on the site.
- **Price alerts**: **🔔** on a wishlist row sets the most you'd pay for that card, typed in the currency you picked. When the daily price update brings it to that price or lower, the app shows a notice once (plus a system notification where allowed), and re-arms if the price climbs back. Games with alerts get their cards loaded after each price check so alerts work without opening them. Kept per device in `AppSettings.priceAlerts`. Logic: `src/shared/priceAlerts.ts`; UI: `PriceAlertEditor.tsx`.
- **Report a bug**: **🐞 Report a bug** at the bottom of the sidebar sends a note (plus, unless unticked, the app version, device, screen and the last few errors from `src/lib/errorLog.ts`) to `deckbuilder_report_bug` in Pawmodoro's `supabase/schema.sql`. Works signed in or not; reports can only be read in the Supabase dashboard (table `deckbuilder_bug_reports`). If sending fails, **Copy report** puts it on the clipboard. Code: `src/shared/bugReport.ts`, `BugReportModal.tsx`.
- **Missing card pictures**: `scripts/missing-art.ts` lists the cards a sync ends up with no picture for, by set and most valuable first; run it locally (`npx rolldown scripts/missing-art.ts --platform node --format esm -o .prices/missing-art.mjs && node .prices/missing-art.mjs riftbound`); riftcodex refuses GitHub Actions' runners, so it isn't part of the daily deploy. Pictures to fill a gap go in `public/card-art/<game>/`.
- **📲 Share the app (phone app)**: in the menu, shows a QR code of the phone app's link (`https://beeftcg-eng.github.io/deckbuilder/`) for a friend to scan with their camera, with the link to copy or send through the phone's share sheet and how to add it to the home screen (`ShareAppModal.tsx`; the code is drawn by `qrcode-generator`, a devDependency for the same reason as `onnxruntime-web` below).
- **📷 Scan cards (phone app)**: from **Collection** or the menu, point the camera at a card and it's recognised down to the exact printing (expansion, collector number, art, rarity), then added to your collection or wishlist with one tap (or automatically, if you switch that on). It reads the card's text on the phone with PaddleOCR (`src/lib/scan/`, models in `src/lib/scan/models/`, about 26 MB downloaded the first time the scanner opens), matches the printed name, set code and collector number, promo numbers included, against the card data (`src/shared/scan/`), and compares the photo with each candidate printing's picture to pick between alternate arts. When printings can't be told apart (the same picture in the card data, a Magic promo stamp, a Yu-Gi-Oh! code printed in several rarities), it says so and shows the others one tap away. It only spends a read on a steady, sharp frame (a card still moving into place is skipped, `FrameGate` in `src/lib/scan/scanner.ts`); the result sheet slides over the camera so the frame never moves; a scan line and "Reading…" show while it works; after repeated unreadable reads of a card with visible text it suggests tilting a shiny card; and the footer shows the engine (GPU/CPU) and the last read's time. A short chime (Web Audio, no sound file) plays when a card is recognised; 🔊 in the top bar mutes it. To stay quick on a phone it reads the name, code and type lines first and only reads the rules text when those weren't enough (`src/shared/scan/priority.ts`), runs the text detector at 640 px, shows the answer as soon as the text is matched while the picture check finishes, and runs the models on the phone's GPU (WebGPU) when it has one that reads a test phrase correctly and quickly, otherwise on the CPU. Phone app only: the desktop build leaves it out (`__SCANNER__` in `vite.config.ts` / `vite.web.config.ts`). With more than one camera, a picker at the top switches between them. `onnxruntime-web` is a devDependency on purpose: Vite bundles what the phone app uses, and as a regular dependency electron-builder would copy its whole 139 MB package into the desktop installer, unused. `onnxruntime-web` is a devDependency on purpose: Vite bundles what the page uses, and as a regular dependency electron-builder would also copy its whole 139 MB package into the installer, unused. `src/shared/scan/scan.test.ts` covers the matching per game.
- **🎴 Practice**: from the deck view (or **Sample hand** in the editor): deal opening hands, **Mulligan** (counted), tap a card to **Discard** it (a discard pile below; tap there to take it back) or to put it **on the bottom** (Magic's London mulligan, Riftbound's replace), **Next turn** (no draw on turn 1 when **On the play**), and an **Odds** tab: tick the cards you care about and see the exact chance of having at least 1-4 of them in your opening hand and by turns 1-5 (hypergeometric, `chanceAtLeast` in `src/shared/sampleHand.ts`).
- **⇄ Compare decks**: from any deck's view (yours or one opened from a share link), see what changes to turn it into another of your decks or into a pasted list (a tournament winner's, a friend's) without importing it: "+2 Back Off, −3 Perched Grimwyrm", zone by zone (runes included), with **Swap** for the other direction and **Copy changes** as text. Printings of a card count together, so a new art isn't a change (`src/shared/deckCompare.ts`, `CompareDecksModal.tsx`).
- **📝 Deck notes**: each deck's view has a Notes box for your sideboard plan, mulligans and matchups (`DeckNotes.tsx`, saved on the deck as `notes`, so it syncs to your phone). Notes work on locked decks too, and are never shown on a share link: the app drops them, and Pawmodoro's `deckbuilder_shared_deck` strips them server-side (re-run `schema.sql` from 2026-09-28 or later).
- **🛡 Sideboard plans**: in a deck's view (games with a sideboard: Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Riftbound), a plan per opponent: the copies that come in from the sideboard and go out of the deck (steppers capped at what the deck has), with a note and a warning when the numbers in and out don't match; opponents you've logged with the deck in Pairings are suggested as you type (`MatchupPlans.tsx`). Saved on the deck as `matchupPlans`, so it syncs; like notes it works on locked decks and is never on a share link (the app reads share links field by field, and Pawmodoro's `deckbuilder_shared_deck` strips it: re-run `schema.sql` from 2026-10-13 or later).
- **Lock a deck**: **🔓 Lock** (in the deck view, the editor header, or the 🔓 on its My Decks card) locks a deck so it can't be changed or deleted by accident: the name, format, cards, icon and delete button are all switched off, and Ctrl+Z won't touch it either. Locked decks show a 🔒 in the sidebar and My Decks. Press **🔒 Locked** to unlock it again. You can still view, export, copy, sample-hand and duplicate a locked deck (the copy starts unlocked).
- **Re-order the games**: drag a game in the sidebar up or down (or focus it and press Alt+↑ / Alt+↓). The order is remembered, and My Decks follows it.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh!**: Main Deck (40–60), Extra Deck (up to 15) and Side Deck (up to 15) with 3 copies by name, Fusion / Synchro / XYZ / Link monsters routed to the Extra Deck automatically, and the **Forbidden / Limited / Semi-Limited** list enforced for the **TCG**, **OCG** and **GOAT (2005)** formats (Forbidden cards can't be added, Limited allows 1 copy, Semi-Limited 2). Export as `Main Deck / Extra Deck / Side Deck` text; import that, a `3x Name` list, or a `.ydk` file (`#main` / `#extra` / `!side` and card passcodes).
- **Re-order your decks**: drag a deck up or down in the sidebar (or use its ▲▼ buttons); the list switches to **My order** and remembers it. Decks you create later appear at the top.
- **🗂 My Decks**: one page with every deck you've built, across all games, as cards showing its icon, game and format, card counts per zone, a legality badge and when you last touched it. Search, filter by game, sort (recent / A–Z / by game / your order), and click a deck to open it (it switches to that game for you).
- **Themes**: eight colour themes (Midnight, Graphite & Amber, Forest, Royal Purple, Crimson, Ocean, Light, Parchment) from the picker at the top of the sidebar. The choice is remembered, applied before the window paints (no flash of the default), and every palette is checked for readable contrast in the tests.
- **📊 Deck stats**: the button in a deck's view opens its tournament stats from Pairings, laid out like Pairings' own deck stats: results logged, win rate, record, win rate going first and going second, each version of the deck (click one, or use the version chips, to see just that version), what it beats and loses to, its record against every opponent, and each event. Going first/second and the version dates and notes need Pairings' `brewhouse_deck_records` from its `master-schema.sql` as of this release; against an older one those parts are simply left out.
- **Welcome tour**: a first launch (no decks yet) walks through the app in ten short steps, outlining each part of the screen, with a language picker on the first card. **👋 Welcome tour** at the bottom of the sidebar replays it; Esc or **Skip tour** ends it, and it doesn't pop up again.
- **English, Spanish or Portuguese**: pick the language from the picker under the theme at the top of the sidebar (the first launch follows your system's language). It switches straight away and is remembered. Card names, card text, card types and set/format names stay as the card data has them, and exported decklist text stays in English so other sites can import it. All UI text lives in `src/shared/i18n/en.ts`, `es.ts` and `pt.ts`: a new string goes in all three (TypeScript and `i18n.test.ts` fail if one is missing).
- **Deck icons**: each deck in the sidebar (and its panel header) shows a thumbnail of a card so you can tell decks apart at a glance. It defaults to the deck's Leader / Legend / Commander, or else its first card that isn't a basic land or Energy, so decks you built earlier get one without any work. Click the **🖼** button on any card in the deck to pick a different one (click it again to go back to the automatic choice; Ctrl+Z undoes it).
- **Collection view**: **▦ Collection** in the sidebar shows what you own for the game you're browsing (search, sort by name / set / copies / value, +/− to adjust) and a **Sets & expansions** tab with your progress in every set. **Add missing** tops you up to owning at least 1–4 of each card in a set (running it twice never overshoots) after a confirmation, and **☆ Wishlist** adds the set's cards you don't own to the wishlist. For Magic a set holds only the cards Scryfall currently shows from it (its data is one printing per card).
- **♻ Spares** (Collection → My cards): the copies you own beyond what a deck can hold (3 of a Riftbound card, 4 of a Magic one, 1 Legend), every printing of a card counted together, with what the spares are worth (the cheapest copies count as the spares) and **Mark for trade** for one card or all of them, so the trade-match alerts can find them (`src/shared/spares.ts`).
- **Delete and undo in bulk** (Collection → My cards): **☑ Select** puts a checkbox on each card (**Select all** takes every card your search matches) to delete them together, and **🗑 Clear…** empties the game's whole collection. **↶ Recent batches** lists the last 20 many-card changes (imports, scanner sessions, a set's **Add missing**, deletions, clears) and **Undo** reverses one whole batch (`src/shared/collectionBatches.ts`); kept on the device, not synced.
- **Import a collection** (**⇪ Import** in Collection): reads a CSV export from TCGplayer, Moxfield, Dragon Shield, ManaBox or a spreadsheet (any file with a name column, plus usually quantity, set and collector number) and adds those copies to your collection. Each row is matched by Scryfall id, TCGplayer product id, set + collector number (only when the name agrees, since apps spell set codes differently), then by name alone; a preview says how many matched, which were matched by name only (printing may differ), which rows are for other games (TCGplayer mixes them), and lists the ones that matched nothing (`src/shared/collectionImport.ts`).
- **🖨 Print proxies**: from a deck, saves a PDF of its cards at real size (2.5 × 3.5 in, 63.5 × 88.9 mm; Yu-Gi-Oh!'s 59 × 86 mm), 9 to a Letter or A4 page with cut marks (on Letter the grid sits 4 mm from the top so the bottom row clears printers' wider bottom margin), for playtesting a list before buying it. Pick the zones, or only the copies you don't own. The PDF asks the viewer to print at actual size (`/PrintScaling /None`). Without a deck: **🖨 Proxies** by Import opens a per-game print list (`src/state/proxyList.ts`, kept on the device until cleared) that you fill by searching, pasting a list, or **🖨 Add to proxies** on a card's detail window; each card can switch printing. The wishlist's **🖨 Print** prints one game's wishlist. The PDF is written in the app (`src/shared/proxies.ts`, pictures drawn at 300 dpi in `src/lib/proxyPdf.ts`); the desktop app opens it in your PDF viewer after saving.
- **🛒 Buy list**: from a deck, the copies you don't own (any printing you own counts), each at the cheapest printing that's the same card (same rules text, so two different Pokémon with one name aren't swapped), or at the deck's own printings, with the total and what the cheap printings save. **Copy for TCGplayer** writes the Mass Entry text (`4 Lightning Bolt [2XM]` for Magic, `4 Name` elsewhere); it can also wishlist the lot (`src/shared/buyList.ts`).
- **Export a collection** (**⇩ Export** in Collection): the game's collection as a CSV with the columns the importer (and most apps) read back, plus price, for-trade and Magic's Scryfall id.
- **Example decks**: with no decks for a game yet, the sidebar and My Decks offer **👀 Open an example deck**, one legal list per game (`src/shared/sampleDecks.ts`) opened read-only like a share link, at its cheapest printings, with **Copy to my decks**.
- **📦 Pack openings** (a Collection tab): log a pack or box with what you paid (typed in your currency; `1,500` and `12,50` both read right), add pulls by search or, in the phone app, with **📷 Scan pulls** (the scanner then adds to that opening), and see what they're worth today against the cost and the best pull. Pulls can go into your collection too. Kept in settings, so in backups but not synced between devices (`src/shared/packOpenings.ts`).
- **📁 Deck folders**: file a deck under a folder from the editor, the deck view or My Decks. The sidebar groups decks by folder (collapsible, remembered per device), and My Decks filters by folder. A folder is just a name on the deck, so it syncs with it, and share links leave it out (`src/shared/deckFolders.ts`).
- **Cheapest printings for a deck**: the Buy list offers **⇄ Use the cheapest printings in the deck** for your own decks: every card you don't own moves to its cheapest printing of the same card that's legal in the deck's format, copies landing on one printing are merged, and it's one Undo (`withCheapestPrintings` in `src/shared/buyList.ts`).
- **Deck value in My Decks**: each deck shows its value and what the copies you don't own would cost, and **Most valuable** sorts by it.
- **Sync that downloads only changes**: the sync engine polls every 20 seconds while the app is on screen, and each poll used to download the whole account (decks, collection, wishlist, binders, items), most of the Supabase project's egress. It now sends the fingerprint of what it already has to `deckbuilder_sync_pull_if_changed` and gets back a few bytes when nothing changed (`src/shared/sync/engine.ts`; mirrors Pawmodoro's `sync_pull_if_changed`). Needs Pawmodoro's `supabase/schema.sql` from 2026-10-13 or later; on an older schema it pulls everything, as before.
- **Share a pack opening**: **📤 Share** on an opening draws a picture of it (name, date, paid vs. worth, best pull, then the pulls as card images, most valuable first, each with its value; the top 60 when there are more) to save or send through the phone's share sheet, or **Copy as text** for every pull (`src/lib/packOpeningImage.ts`).
- **Pack openings, price alerts and the value graph sync** between your devices: one row per item in Pawmodoro's `deckbuilder_user_items` (a pack opening by id, an alert by card, a value point by game and day). Every settings save is diffed so only changed items are queued (`src/shared/sync/items.ts`); a pull rebuilds them, and the first pull merges instead, so what was only on this device is uploaded rather than lost. Servers without the table leave local items alone.
- **Trade match alerts**: while your trade profile is public, new matches (someone now has a card you want, or wants one you have for trade) are announced once, in the app and as a notification, checked at launch and when you come back to the app at most every 3 hours (`src/shared/tradeAlerts.ts`). Opening Trade → Matches counts as having seen them.
- **Keyboard shortcuts (desktop)**: `/` card search, `+`/`−` on the card under the mouse, `Ctrl+Z` undo, `Esc` closes the top window, `?` lists them (App.tsx, `src/lib/shortcuts.ts`).
- **Languages**: English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese (`src/shared/i18n/`), picked from the system/browser language until you choose one; `i18n.test.ts` checks every translation has every key and message argument. Reais are formatted Brazilian-style.
- **Export**: decks and the wishlist can each be copied to the clipboard as plain text, saved as `.txt`, uploaded for a shareable paste link (via [dpaste.com](https://dpaste.com), no account needed), or rendered as one picture (a `.jpg`, always under 12 MB so it sends through WhatsApp's 16 MB limit). A Riftbound deck's text is exactly what Riot's event locator (deck submission) and Piltover Archive import: `Legend:` / `Champion:` / `MainDeck:` / `Battlefields:` / `Runes:` / `Sideboard:` headings with plain `3 Card Name` lines (comma titles, no printing tags, runes as `6 Order Rune`) and no title lines above them. The chosen Champion is the Champion matching your Legend (else the first one in the Main Deck), listed under `Champion:` and left out of the 39-card `MainDeck:`. The picture puts as many cards in a row as it takes to show the whole deck in one look (about as wide as it is tall), with each zone's card count in its heading, ×N badges and the total in the header; the Save button shows the file's size.
- **✦ Foil and condition**: each row in Collection → My cards has **✦ Finish…** to say how many copies are foil (or etched) and which aren't Near Mint. Copies without a detail are regular Near Mint, so nothing changes for a collection that never uses it. Foil copies count at the card's foil price where the source has one (Scryfall's `usd_foil`; TCGplayer's Foil / Holofoil rows in the daily price files, `foilPrices`), in the collection total, the value graph and the value sort. CSV imports read ManaBox/Moxfield's Foil column, TCGplayer and Dragon Shield's Printing, and conditions ("Near Mint", "Lightly Played Holofoil"...); the export writes a row per finish and condition (`src/shared/copyDetails.ts`). Kept with the settings and synced as `copy_detail` items (needs `scripts/pawmodoro-migration-2026-09-30.sql` on the server).
- **🕘 Deck history**: a deck keeps its earlier lists. The first change after a 30-minute break saves the list as it stood, and **Save current list** in **🕘 History** saves one with a name ("after regionals"). Pick a version to see what changed since, **Go back to this list** (the current list is saved first, and Undo reverses it), rename or delete it. Up to 30 per deck, unnamed ones dropped first; synced with the deck, never on a share link (`src/shared/deckHistory.ts`).
- **🏷 Card tags**: in a deck's List view, 🏷 on a card sets your own tags ("ramp, removal, draw"), with the deck's other tags one tap away; every printing of the card shares them. **By tag** groups the deck view by them, a card under each of its tags. Synced with the deck and shown on share links; Archidekt categories become tags on import (`src/shared/deckTags.ts`).
- **⟳ Rotation warnings**: My Decks and the deck view show how many of a deck's cards leave at the next rotation, with the list on hover. Magic's Standard uses Scryfall's upcoming-Standard legality (so it knows once a rotation is announced); One Piece and Riftbound use the **Next rotation** date and leaving sets you tick in the ban list editor (`src/shared/rotation.ts`).
- **Import from a link**: paste an Archidekt (Magic), Limitless (Pokémon) or One Piece Limitless deck link into Import and press **⇩ Get the deck from this link**. Archidekt decks keep their exact printings, zones and categories. The desktop app reads the page in the main process (`electron/ipc/deckUrl.ts`, only those sites); those sites don't let web pages read them, so the phone app says to paste the list instead. Moxfield blocks programs outright: use its Export → Copy (`src/shared/deckUrl.ts`).
- **Riftbound decks from Piltover Archive**: a `piltoverarchive.com/decks/view/…` link imports the Legend, Main Deck (Champions included), Battlefields, Runes and Sideboard, each card at the exact printing the deck uses when this catalog has it (matched by TCGplayer id, `fromPiltoverArchive` in `src/shared/deckUrl.ts`). Desktop app only, like the other links: the site doesn't let web pages read it.
- **🌐 Community lists (Riftbound)**: **🌐 Lists** in the sidebar shows recent tournament decks from [RiftAtlas](https://riftatlas.com/decks): its Decks page plus every deck in the standings of each recent event's page (over a thousand lists across a dozen events; `src/shared/communityLists.ts`). RiftAtlas doesn't let web pages read it, so the daily phone-app deploy collects them (`scripts/build-community-lists.ts`) and publishes `community/riftbound.json` next to the app; every app reads that, and the desktop app reads RiftAtlas itself when the file is missing or over a day and a half old. The last lists read are kept on the device, shown straight away and used offline ("Offline: showing the lists from …"; `src/state/communityLists.ts`). Lists are grouped by event, or sorted by **Most owned** / **Cheapest to finish**; each shows its Legend's art, domains, a placing medal, and how much of it you own with what the rest costs at the cheapest printings. Open one to see its cards, then **Import…**, **⇄ Compare with…** one of your decks, **Copy list**, **🔔 Watch price** (told once, in the app and as a notification, when it costs your target or less to finish, checked whenever prices update, like price alerts) or **🤝 Who trades these?** (which of its missing cards other traders have marked for trade; reads Browse only when pressed). The **Meta** tab ranks the Legends by recent finishes (lists, wins, top 4s) with **your record against each** from your Pairings results (opponents matched by champion name); pick one to see how many of its lists play each card at its usual number of copies, import its newest list, or **Start a deck from the core** (cards in at least half the lists, runes split the usual way; `src/shared/communityMeta.ts`). The window's pieces are in `src/components/community/`.
- **RiftAtlas check**: the same deploy step is a daily check that RiftAtlas can still be read. If its pages change and the lists can't be read (or come back too few, or with deck codes that don't decode), the workflow re-publishes the last good file and opens a GitHub issue, which it closes again once a deploy reads them. The window also says when the page can't be read, and the error goes in the bug report's log.
- **📈 Against the meta** (Riftbound deck view): opens to compare the deck with the recent community lists for its Legend: the cards at least half of them play that it doesn't (with how many copies they usually run), and its picks a tenth of them or fewer make (`DeckMetaCompare.tsx`, `compareToMeta` in `src/shared/communityMeta.ts`). Closed until opened, so the lists are only read when wanted.
- **Your events** (a Community lists tab): the Legends you actually face, from the opponents in your Pairings results with your Riftbound decks, most played first, with your record against each; one opens its Meta page. Opponents that don't name one Legend are listed by name (`localMeta`, `community/CommunityEventsView.tsx`).
- **Rising and falling cards**: a Legend's page in the Meta tab shows the cards whose share of its lists moved most between the last two weeks (counted back from its newest list) and before, when both sides have at least five lists (`cardTrends`).
- **New lists for your Legends (opt-in)**: **🔕 Tell me about new lists** on a Legend in the Meta tab (or in a deck's Against the meta) turns on a notice, and a notification, the next time the app opens after RiftAtlas has lists with that Legend from an event it hadn't seen ("14 new Kennen lists from CCS Qualifier #6"). Off for every Legend until you turn it on; opting in counts the events already there as seen, and the app only looks for them at startup when at least one Legend is on (`newListsFor`, settings `listAlerts`).
- **Deck code as a QR code**: Export's **Show as QR code** (Riftbound) draws the deck code to scan with a phone, for an event's registration, TCG Arena or another device.
- **Start from what's winning**: a Riftbound deck with its Legend picked and nothing else yet offers RiftAtlas's newest list with that Legend, or the core of its recent lists, to fill it (`src/components/CommunityStartHint.tsx`); Undo puts it back.
- **Riftbound deck codes**: paste a deck code (the string TCG Arena, RiftAtlas and Piltover Archive share decks as) into Import and press **⇩ Read the deck code**; **Copy deck code** in Export goes the other way. `src/shared/riftboundDeckCode.ts` reads and writes versions 1-6 the way RiftAtlas's own encoder does (codes round-trip byte for byte), with alternate arts and Signatures kept, printings a code can't name (promo sets) written as a printing of the same card it can, and runes as Origins' rune of each domain.
- **A crash stays in its corner**: the sidebar, card browser, the panel on the right, the scanner and share-link view each sit in an error boundary (`src/components/ErrorBoundary.tsx`), so a bug shows "Something went wrong here" with **Try again** and **🐞 Report a bug** instead of a blank window. Switching screen or deck retries by itself.
- **Launch smoke test**: with `DECKBUILDER_SMOKE_TEST=1` the desktop app starts on an empty data folder and exits 1 if its window crashes, the page throws, a panel shows "Something went wrong", or its screen doesn't appear in 45 seconds (`electron/lib/smokeTest.ts`). CI runs it on Linux under `xvfb-run`, and the Windows build runs the built app this way before the installer can be published.
- **A lighter phone-app start**: Spanish and Portuguese load when they're used (`loadLanguage` in `src/shared/i18n/index.ts`; the desktop main process imports `i18n/all`), the Yu-Gi-Oh! artwork map with Yu-Gi-Oh!'s card data (`loadArtMap`), and the Riftbound community code with a Riftbound deck or the Community lists window. The first load went from 239 KB to 185 KB gzipped.
- **Ban lists that update themselves**: the daily deploy publishes `banlists.json` (`scripts/build-ban-lists.ts`). One Piece's and Riftbound's lists are kept in this repo; Yu-Gi-Oh!'s TCG, OCG and GOAT Forbidden/Limited/Semi-Limited lists come from YGOPRODeck. Every app reads the file at launch. One Piece and Riftbound lists are merged into your saved ones, keeping cards you banned or unbanned yourself in the ban list editor. Yu-Gi-Oh!'s list is laid over your card data, with no re-download. A list that changed shows once: what moved, and which of your decks it makes illegal (`BanListUpdateModal.tsx`). Games whose legality comes with the card data (Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic, Pokémon) get **View the list…** under the legality panel.
- **🌐 Community lists for One Piece and Pokémon**: the Lists window, Meta, Against the meta, suggestions and (One Piece) "Start from what's winning" now cover One Piece and Pokémon too. The lists are the top 16 of each recent Standard event on [Limitless](https://play.limitlesstcg.com) (16+ players for One Piece, 32+ for Pokémon), collected daily into `community/<game>.json`. One Piece lists are grouped by Leader (card number, since Leaders share names). Pokémon lists are grouped by Limitless's archetype, and your deck is matched to the archetype whose lists share the most of its cards. What differs per game lives in `src/shared/communityGames.ts`.
- **🔎 Ctrl+K, one search box for everything**: your decks in every game, the current game's cards, and the app's actions: the open deck's Export, Practice, Proxies, Buy list, Compare, History, Share and stats; the panels; new deck, import, Lists, scan, print proxies; switching game or theme; syncing, account, tour, patch notes, bug report. Also **🔎 Go to…** in the sidebar (the More menu on phones). Windows owned by other components open through `src/lib/commands.ts`.
- **🎴 Practice plays cards out**: tap **Play** to put a card in play and pay its cost from the turn's resources (`src/shared/practiceResources.ts`). One Piece gets DON!!: 1 going first or 2 going second, then +2 a turn, up to 10. Riftbound gets runes: 2 a turn, 3 on the second player's first turn, up to 12. Magic counts the lands you've played; Pokémon counts the Energy attached. Cards you can't afford this turn are dimmed, and a card in play taps back to your hand with its cost refunded.
- **Share anonymous usage counts** (opt-in, off until ticked, bottom of the sidebar): counts which windows, panels and games get used, by fixed names only, and sends each finished day's totals with the platform and version. No account, device id, deck, card or typed text. **What's sent?** shows everything waiting on the device (`src/shared/usage.ts`; the server side is `scripts/pawmodoro-migration-2026-11-13-usage.sql`).
